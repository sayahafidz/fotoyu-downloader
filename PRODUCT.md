# Product Context

## Register
Product: a browser-based Fotoyu photo selection, preview, and download tool.

## Users and Purpose
Users open photo lists on phones or desktops, inspect individual photos at a larger size, select images, and save individual files or browser-generated ZIP archives. Production hosting is Vercel.

## Personality and Direction
The owner's current direction is a native-feeling, mobile-first redesign. The experience should be practical, familiar, photo-led, and responsive. Use clear touch controls, thumb-reachable primary actions, restrained styling, and concise Indonesian copy.

## Principles
Prioritize reliable photo loading and downloads. Make loading, failures, and retry actions visible. Maintain usable controls on mobile and keyboard access on desktop. Do not disguise a failed request as a successful save. Prefer browser-side processing for batch archives and streamed server responses for images.

## Anti-references
Avoid generic AI-generated styling, decorative controls, unsupported claims, and extra steps between selecting a photo and saving it.
